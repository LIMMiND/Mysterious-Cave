🕯️ Mysterious Cave

 A pixel-art roguelike dungeon crawler written in Python with pygame. Procedurally generated floors, turn-based combat, quests, a shop, a boss every 5 floors and a rare axe weapon. All graphics are drawn in code, no image assets. Built as a first game project with the help of AI (Claude).

✨ Possibilities

- **Infinite Floors** — a new map every time: rooms of various shapes, corridors, traps, barrels, fountains.
- **7 types of enemies** — goblin, bat, slime, skeleton archer, sorcerer, ogre, and Goblin King (boss).
- **The Fog of War and the Torch** — only what remains is visible; the map is revealed as you explore.
- **Quests and Shop** — Elders provide tasks, while a merchant sells potions, scrolls, and upgrades.
- **Leveling up** — levels, hero, blade sharpening, lantern oil, crit chance.
- **The armor visible on the hero** — each level of chainmail adds a new element: pauldrons, a breastplate, a helmet, a cloak, and a golden set.
- **Two weapons** — a sword and a rare battle-axe (dropped by an ogre): strikes in an arc across 3 squares, but with a one-turn delay.
- **Animations** — hero stance, walking, and attacking; smooth UI transitions; impact effects.
- **Music** — separate tracks for the menu and boss fight.
- **Records** — are preserved between runs.
